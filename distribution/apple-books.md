# Apple Books

## Ploos policy

- Canonical author: **Per Gustav Ousdal**
- Canonical publisher: **Ploos AS**
- Primary ebook artifact: **EPUB**
- Every submitted EPUB must pass the latest EPUBCheck.
- Store metadata and cover must match canonical publication metadata.
- Apple-specific identifiers are distribution identifiers and do not replace ISBN.

## Release gate

1. Canonical metadata validated.
2. EPUBCheck passes with no errors.
3. Apple Books rendering reviewed.
4. Cover, title and author match canonical metadata.
5. Description, categories, rights/territories and price reviewed.
6. Apple Books identifier and publication status recorded after publication.

Official guidance:
- https://authors.apple.com/support/4574-publish-book-from-web
