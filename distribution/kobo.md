# Kobo Writing Life

## Ploos policy

- Canonical author: **Per Gustav Ousdal**
- Canonical publisher: **Ploos AS**
- Primary ebook artifact: **EPUB**
- Upload the validated EPUB rather than relying on store-side document conversion.
- Store metadata must match canonical publication metadata and cover.
- Record the Kobo product identifier after publication.
- Where an official Ploos ebook ISBN is assigned, use it consistently for that publication product.

## Release gate

1. Canonical metadata validated.
2. EPUB validation passes with no errors.
3. Kobo rendering/testing completed.
4. Cover title/author matches metadata.
5. Publisher, language, categories and synopsis reviewed.
6. Rights, territory, DRM choice and price recorded.
7. Kobo publication status/product identifier recorded after publication.
